TERMS OF PAYMENT

Welcome to CTBS CINEMAS's E-ticketing system!

At the moment the promotions for students, seniors and children are not applied in the E-ticketing system. If you want to take advantage of these promotions and discounts, please buy your tickets at our BOX OFFICE counters instead.

Thank you very much and we hope you have wonderful cinematic experiences at CTBS CINEMAS!

Several notes to keep in mind before making online payments:

  1. Your debit or credit card's online payment service must have been activated, and the card's balance must be enough to make the payment. You need to fill in the card information as it is (the account holder's name, the card number, the expiration date, the CVC and OTP numbers, etc.)
  2. Purchased tickets are non-cancellable, non-exchangeable and non-refundable for any reasons. We only issue refunds if your card has been billed but our system has not registered your reservation, and you have not received a ticket reservation confirmation.
  3. Within 30 minutes after you have made a successful payment, we will send you a ticket reservation confirmation via email. If you need help or have any questions and complaints, please contact Facebook Fanpage CTBS Cinemas within 60 minutes after you have made a successful payment. After this timeframe, we would not resolve any complaints.
  4. We are not responsible for cases in which you fill in the wrong email, phone number and as a result do not receive the ticket reservation confirmation. Please check your information carefully before making a payment. We neither resolve nor take responsibility for cases in which we have emailed you the ticket reservation confirmation but for some reason you cannot go see the film (noshow).
  5. Please check your reservation confirmation carefully and remember your reservation code. To receive your ticket from our BOX OFFICE counters, you also need to bring a form of identification (such as your national ID card, your student card, your passport, etc.)
  6. According to the regulations of the Cinema Department, certain films are not for audience under the age of 13, or 16, or 18. When you receive your tickets at the BOX OFFICE counters, the staff might ask you for proof that you are old enough to watch an age-restricted film. Please keep this in mind when you purchase your tickets.
  7. In some cases of force majeure, the movie session can be cancelled to ensure the best safety for customers. The transaction amount will be refunded directly to your bank account which was used to buy tickets online. We will contact you through your contact information in the Member info. description, to get your confirmation. Depending on your bank account and payment procedures, the refund process may get the different period following:

    ATM cards: maximum 1 working week

    Visa Credit Card, Master Card, JCB Card: maximum 1 working month

    Vietcombank's cards: maximum 48 working hours
